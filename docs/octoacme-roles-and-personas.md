# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define quality strategy and ensure delivered work meets functional and non-functional expectations before release.

### Primary Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, test scope, and quality gates
- Align test coverage with acceptance criteria and risk areas
- Coordinate defect triage, prioritization, and verification
- Advise on release readiness based on quality signals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Collaborate on testability, automation coverage, and defect resolution
- **Product Managers:** Clarify acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **Project Managers:** Communicate quality risks, test milestones, and release readiness status

### Contribution Across Delivery Lifecycle
- **Planning:** Defines quality goals, test approach, and entry/exit criteria
- **Execution:** Tracks test progress, defects, and validation outcomes
- **Release:** Confirms quality gates and supports go/no-go recommendations
- **Continuous Improvement:** Leads defect trend reviews and test process improvements

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders / Sponsors provide strategic direction, funding context, and decision support to keep initiatives aligned with business goals.

### Primary Responsibilities
- Set strategic objectives and success outcomes
- Approve major scope, timeline, and investment decisions
- Provide escalation support for cross-team blockers
- Validate delivered outcomes against business expectations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide business context for critical trade-offs when needed
- **Product Managers:** Align priorities, outcomes, and roadmap direction
- **Project Managers:** Review status, risks, dependencies, and decision points

### Contribution Across Delivery Lifecycle
- **Planning:** Confirms priorities, constraints, and business outcomes
- **Execution:** Provides timely decisions for escalations and trade-offs
- **Release:** Validates readiness from a business and customer impact perspective
- **Continuous Improvement:** Sponsors retrospective actions with measurable business impact

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide technical direction to ensure solutions are scalable, secure, maintainable, and aligned with product goals.

### Primary Responsibilities
- Define architecture and technical standards for delivery
- Evaluate technical options, dependencies, and implementation risks
- Guide decomposition of work into feasible increments
- Mentor developers and support design/code review quality

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide technical direction, unblock design decisions, and coach implementation approaches
- **Product Managers:** Shape feasible scope and clarify trade-offs between value, cost, and complexity
- **Project Managers:** Surface technical dependencies, sequencing constraints, and risk mitigation plans

### Contribution Across Delivery Lifecycle
- **Planning:** Drives technical discovery, estimates, and architecture decisions
- **Execution:** Monitors implementation quality, integration risks, and technical debt
- **Release:** Ensures operational readiness, rollback plans, and supportability
- **Continuous Improvement:** Identifies architecture improvements and engineering process refinements

---

## Release Manager / Deployment Lead

### Role Summary
Release Managers / Deployment Leads coordinate release execution so deployments are predictable, communicated, and recoverable.

### Primary Responsibilities
- Maintain release plans, cutover checklists, and deployment schedules
- Coordinate environment readiness and release dependencies
- Manage release communications and stakeholder updates
- Ensure rollback and incident response paths are defined

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Coordinate packaging, deployment sequencing, and rollback procedures
- **Product Managers:** Align release scope and timing with customer commitments
- **Project Managers:** Synchronize release milestones, risks, and cross-team dependencies

### Contribution Across Delivery Lifecycle
- **Planning:** Defines release calendar, readiness criteria, and deployment governance
- **Execution:** Tracks release tasks, validates prerequisites, and coordinates handoffs
- **Release:** Leads go-live activities, communications, and contingency execution
- **Continuous Improvement:** Reviews release outcomes to improve deployment reliability

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
Business Analysts / Requirements Leads translate business needs into clear, testable requirements that teams can execute with confidence.

### Primary Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and refine business and functional requirements
- Define acceptance criteria and requirement traceability
- Resolve ambiguities, assumptions, and dependency gaps
- Support change impact analysis and requirement updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Clarify requirements detail, edge cases, and constraints
- **Product Managers:** Align requirement definitions with product outcomes and priorities
- **Project Managers:** Keep requirement milestones and scope decisions transparent

### Contribution Across Delivery Lifecycle
- **Planning:** Produces clear requirements, acceptance criteria, and scope boundaries
- **Execution:** Supports implementation clarifications and change control decisions
- **Release:** Verifies delivered capabilities map to approved requirements
- **Continuous Improvement:** Uses delivery feedback to improve requirement quality and flow
